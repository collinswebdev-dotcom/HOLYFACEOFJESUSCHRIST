🎙️ Talks on the Holy Face

Each talk is a prayer. Each word is an offering. May the Holy Face of Jesus bless you, heal you, and draw you closer to His Sacred Heart.

Talk 1: The Face of Mercy

Duration: 15 minutes | Series: Encountering the Holy Face

In this first talk, we explore the relationship between the Holy Face and Divine Mercy. How the gaze of Jesus upon the sinner is not one of condemnation, but of infinite compassion. The Face that looked upon Peter after his denial looks upon us today.

Talk 2: The Veil of Veronica

Duration: 18 minutes | Series: Encountering the Holy Face

Veronica's act of courage and compassion gave us one of the most powerful relics of the Passion. This talk reflects on what it means to console the suffering Christ and how our small acts of love leave an imprint on His Heart.

Talk 3: The Face of Suffering

Duration: 20 minutes | Series: Encountering the Holy Face

The Holy Face is the face of every suffering person. When we serve the poor, the sick, and the marginalized, we are gazing into the Face of Christ. This talk challenges us to see Jesus in the suffering other.

Talk 4: Reparation and the Golden Arrow

Duration: 22 minutes | Series: Encountering the Holy Face

Why does God ask for reparation? This talk delves into the mystery of the Golden Arrow prayer and how our acts of reparation console the wounded Heart of Christ and heal the wounds of the Church.

Talk 5: The Hidden Face in the Eucharist

Duration: 25 minutes | Series: Encountering the Holy Face

The Holy Face is hidden in the Most Holy Sacrament of the Altar. This talk explores the intimate connection between the Holy Face devotion and Eucharistic adoration. In the Host, we gaze upon the hidden Face of God.

Talk 6: The Face of the Resurrection

Duration: 20 minutes | Series: Encountering the Holy Face

The Face that was disfigured in the Passion is now radiant in the Resurrection. This talk explores the glory that awaits us when we contemplate the Holy Face in eternity. What we see dimly now, we shall see face to face.
