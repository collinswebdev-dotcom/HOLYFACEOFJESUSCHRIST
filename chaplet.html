📿 The Holy Face Chaplet

Revealed to Blessed Maria Pierina De Micheli in 1936. A powerful devotion of reparation for the offenses against the Holy Face of Jesus.

How to Pray the Chaplet

On a regular Rosary, use the 33 small beads to honor the 33 years of Our Lord's earthly life.

Opening Prayer

O Jesus, who in Thy bitter Passion didst become the "Man of Sorrows," and wast crowned with thorns, grant us the grace to make reparation for the sins committed against Thy Holy Face.

Amen.

On the Large Beads (6 times)

O Jesus, who said, "Learn of Me for I am meek and humble of heart," make our hearts like unto Thine.

Amen.

On the Small Beads (33 times)

O Jesus, show us Thy Holy Face, and we shall be saved.

Amen.

Closing Prayer

O God, our Protector, look upon the Face of Thy Christ, and have mercy on us. Through Christ our Lord.

Amen.

The Golden Arrow Prayer

May the most holy, most sacred, most adorable, most incomprehensible and unutterable Name of God be always praised, blessed, loved, adored and glorified in heaven, on earth and under the earth, by all the creatures of God, and by the Sacred Heart of Our Lord Jesus Christ in the Most Holy Sacrament of the Altar.

Amen.
