Act of Consecration to the Holy Face

O Jesus, through the Holy Face, I consecrate myself entirely to You.

I offer You my eyes, that they may look only upon what is holy and pleasing to You.

I offer You my ears, that they may hear only Your voice and the cry of the poor.

I offer You my mouth, that it may speak only words of love, truth, and reparation.

I offer You my hands, that they may do only works of mercy and service.

I offer You my feet, that they may walk only in the path of Your commandments.

I offer You my heart, that it may love only You and those You place before me.

I offer You my mind, that it may dwell only on what is true, beautiful, and good.

I offer You my body, a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to You.

Accept my offering, O Lord, and let Your Holy Face be my light, my strength, and my salvation.

Amen.

Morning Offering to the Holy Face

O Holy Face of Jesus, I offer You this day—all my prayers, works, joys, and sufferings. Unite them to Your sacred Passion and offer them to the Father for the salvation of souls.

May Your countenance shine upon me, guide me, and protect me. In every decision, let me seek Your Face. In every trial, let me remember Your suffering. In every joy, let me give You glory.

Amen.

Evening Surrender to the Holy Face

O Jesus, as this day ends, I return to You. For all that I have done well, I give You thanks. For all that I have done poorly, I ask Your mercy.

Hide me in the shelter of Your Holy Face. Let me rest in the shadow of Your wounds. Grant me peaceful sleep, and if I should not wake, bring me at last to behold Your Face in eternal glory.

Amen.
