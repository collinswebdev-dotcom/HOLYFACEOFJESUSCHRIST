⏳ Holy Hour of Reparation

A guided hour of adoration and reparation before the Holy Face of Jesus. Find a quiet place, light a candle, and enter into His presence.

0:00

Opening: Sign of the Cross

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.

O Holy Face of Jesus, we come before You in adoration. We come to console You for the offenses of the world and to make reparation for the sins committed against Your Sacred Countenance.

0:05

Hymn of Adoration: O Salutaris Hostia

O salutaris Hostia, quae caeli pandis ostium. Bella premunt hostilia, da robur, fer auxilium.

O saving Victim, opening wide the gate of heaven. Hostile wars press upon us; give strength, bring aid.

0:10

The Golden Arrow Prayer (5 times)

May the most holy, most sacred, most adorable, most incomprehensible and unutterable Name of God be always praised, blessed, loved, adored and glorified in heaven, on earth and under the earth, by all the creatures of God, and by the Sacred Heart of Our Lord Jesus Christ in the Most Holy Sacrament of the Altar. Amen.

0:20

Scripture Reading: Isaiah 53:2-5

"He had no form or majesty that we should look at Him, and no beauty that we should desire Him. He was despised and rejected by men; a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief... Surely He has borne our griefs and carried our sorrows... But He was pierced for our transgressions; He was crushed for our iniquities."

0:30

Silent Adoration

Gaze upon the Holy Face in silence. Let His eyes meet yours. What is He saying to you?

0:40

The Litany of the Holy Face (abbreviated)

Face of Jesus, disfigured in the Passion — have mercy on us.
Face of Jesus, crowned with thorns — have mercy on us.
Face of Jesus, covered with spittle — have mercy on us.
Face of Jesus, imprinted on Veronica's veil — have mercy on us.
Face of Jesus, radiant in the Resurrection — have mercy on us.

0:50

Act of Consecration to the Holy Face

O Jesus, through the Holy Face, I consecrate myself entirely to You. I offer You my eyes, to look only upon what is holy. I offer You my mouth, to speak only words of love. I offer You my heart, to love only You. Accept my offering, O Lord, and let Your Holy Face be my light and my salvation. Amen.

0:55

Closing Hymn: Tantum Ergo

Tantum ergo Sacramentum, veneremur cernui. Et antiquum documentum, novo cedat ritui. Praestet fides supplementum, sensuum defectui.

Down in adoration falling, lo, the sacred Host we hail. Lo, o'er ancient forms departing, newer rites of grace prevail. Faith for all defects supplying, where the feeble senses fail.

1:00

Final Blessing & Sign of the Cross

May the Holy Face of Jesus shine upon you and protect you. May He hide you in the shelter of His wounds and bring you at last to contemplate His glory forever.

In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit. Amen.
