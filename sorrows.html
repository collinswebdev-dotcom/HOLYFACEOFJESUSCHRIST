💔 Seven Sorrows of the Holy Face

Meditate on the seven sorrows endured by the Holy Face of Jesus during His bitter Passion.

I

The Agony in the Garden

"And being in agony, He prayed more earnestly; and His sweat became like great drops of blood falling down to the ground." — Luke 22:44

The Holy Face of Jesus was bathed in bloody sweat as He contemplated the weight of the world's sins. His face, once radiant with glory, was now contorted with the anguish of bearing our iniquities. He saw each soul, each sin, and each sorrow. And He accepted it all for love of us.

II

The Betrayal Kiss

"Judas, would you betray the Son of Man with a kiss?" — Luke 22:48

The Holy Face received the kiss of betrayal from one of His own disciples. The lips that praised Him now delivered Him to death. Jesus looked into the face of Judas with love, even as He was handed over to sinners. The Holy Face absorbed the pain of betrayal from friends and followers.

III

The Blindfolding and Striking

"They blindfolded Him and asked, 'Prophesy! Who is it that struck you?'" — Luke 22:64

The Holy Face was covered, struck, and mocked. The eyes that gazed with compassion upon the multitudes were hidden in darkness. The face that turned away demons was now the target of human cruelty. Each blow was an insult to the Divine Majesty.

IV

The Crown of Thorns

"And they twisted together a crown of thorns and put it on His head." — Matthew 27:29

The Holy Face was crowned with thorns, the sharp spikes piercing His brow and sending streams of blood down His cheeks. The King of Kings was mocked with a crown of suffering. His Sacred Head, once cradled by His Mother, was now a garden of pain.

V

The Spitting and Mockery

"And they spit on Him and took the reed and struck Him on the head." — Matthew 27:30

The Holy Face, the most beautiful of all faces, was covered with the spittle of sinners. The mouth that spoke the words of eternal life was defiled. The face that the angels adore was treated with the utmost contempt. Yet He did not turn away.

VI

Veronica's Veil

"And as they led Him away, they seized one Simon of Cyrene... and laid on him the cross." — Luke 23:26

A woman named Veronica, moved with compassion, wiped the Holy Face with her veil. The Sacred Countenance, covered in blood, sweat, and dust, left its image on the cloth. In the midst of His agony, Jesus rewarded this act of love by leaving His likeness—a testament to His gratitude for those who console Him.

VII

The Crucifixion

"And Jesus cried out again with a loud voice and yielded up His spirit." — Matthew 27:50

The Holy Face, bruised, bloodied, and unrecognizable, was lifted up on the cross for all to see. The eyes that had wept for Lazarus now closed in death. The face that once shone with the glory of Tabor was now extinguished. But from this death, the world would receive life. The Holy Face, in its ultimate suffering, became the source of our salvation.
