Why This Website Exists

This website was created as a voice in the silence.

During my novitiate year, my phone will be taken away. I will be immersed in prayer, silence, and formation. But I did not want the talks on the Holy Face of Jesus to stop.

So I built this — a place where souls can come to encounter the Holy Face. A place where the love of Jesus continues to speak, even when I cannot.

Each talk is a prayer. Each word is an offering. May the Holy Face of Jesus bless you, heal you, and draw you closer to His Sacred Heart.

🕊️ The Mission

To spread devotion to the Holy Face of Jesus Christ through prayer, education, and community. To console the wounded Heart of Christ by making reparation for the sins of the world. To lead souls to the healing gaze of the Savior.

📿 What You Will Find Here

The Holy Face Chaplet — a powerful prayer of reparation.
The Litany of the Holy Face — invoking the Sacred Countenance.
The Golden Arrow — fire your prayer to heaven.
The Seven Sorrows — meditations on the Passion.
A Virtual Candle Wall — light a candle for your intentions.
A guided Holy Hour — for adoration and reparation.
The history of the Shroud of Turin — the relic that reveals the Holy Face.
Sacred videos — gaze upon the Holy Face with music and silence.

🙏 A Request

If this website has touched your heart, please pray for me during my postulancy and novitiate. And if you feel called, share this website with someone who needs to encounter the Holy Face of Jesus.
